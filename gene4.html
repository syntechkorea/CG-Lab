Turritopsis(Turritopsis dohrnii)

Information

Turritopsis dohrnii, also known as the "immortal jellyfish," is a small species of jellyfish capable of reverting its cells to an earlier stage of development.
This unique ability allows it to potentially bypass death and start its life cycle anew.

Overview

The genome of Turritopsis dohrnii has been sequenced to understand the genetic basis of its unique regenerative capabilities.
Researchers are investigating the genes and pathways that enable this jellyfish to revert to a juvenile state,
which may provide insights into cellular regeneration and aging processes in other organisms.
