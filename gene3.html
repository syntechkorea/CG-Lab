Axolotl(Ambystoma mexicanum)

Information

The axolotl is a type of salamander known for its remarkable regenerative abilities.
Unlike most amphibians, it retains its larval features throughout its life, including external gills and a tail fin.

Overview

The axolotl genome has been sequenced to understand the genetic basis of its regenerative capabilities.
Researchers have identified genes and pathways that are crucial for tissue regeneration,
which may have implications for regenerative medicine in humans.
