Bowhead Whale(Balaena mysticetus)

information

Bowhead whales are large Arctic whales known for their massive skulls and long baleen plates.
They are among the longest-living mammals, with some individuals living over 200 years.

Overview

The bowhead whale genome was sequenced to understand the genetic basis of their longevity and resistance to age-related diseases.
Researchers have identified unique genetic adaptations that contribute to their extended lifespan and healthspan.
